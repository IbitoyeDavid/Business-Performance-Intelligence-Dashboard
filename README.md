# Business Performance Intelligence Dashboard

## Overview

This project is an interactive Business Performance Intelligence Dashboard developed in Microsoft Power BI to analyse sales, profitability, customer segments, products, regions, channels, and operational performance.

The dashboard was designed to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that can support management decision-making.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

The Executive Overview provides a high-level view of overall business performance.

Key metrics include:

- Net Sales
- Gross Profit
- Gross Profit Margin
- Net Profit
- Sales Target Achievement
- Year-over-Year Sales Growth

The page also analyses sales performance by region, customer segment, and time period.

### 2. Sales & Profit Analysis

This page provides a detailed analysis of sales and profitability across different business dimensions.

It includes:

- Net Sales by Category
- Net Sales by Channel
- Net Sales by Region
- Gross Profit by Category
- Gross Profit by Channel
- Net Sales by Product
- Employee Sales Performance

### 3. Management Analysis

The Management Analysis page focuses on operational and management-level performance.

Key metrics include:

- Return Order Rate
- Cancellation Rate
- Pending Order Rate
- Net Profit
- Net Profit Margin
- Returned Orders by Product
- Approved Expenses
- Sales and Profitability by Category
